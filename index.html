About LOCAYTER

Better targeting starts with the property.

LOCAYTER is a property intelligence platform built for businesses whose customers can be identified, in part, by the physical characteristics of their homes. We combine satellite-image analysis, configurable search criteria and human verification to help commercial teams focus their prospecting.
